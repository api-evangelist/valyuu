# Valyuu

> Valyuu is an Amsterdam-founded embedded recommerce service provider: a plug-and-play trade-in platform businesses integrate to buy back, resell, and recycle used consumer electronics (smartphones, tablets, smartwatches) in Dutch, German, and English markets. A Techstars portfolio company. As of mid-2026, valyuu.com redirects to prioont.com, whose storefront is currently unreachable; the Partner API's documented staging host is also down.
